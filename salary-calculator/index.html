How we calculate this

Our calculator uses the official HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) rates and thresholds for the 2025/26 tax year. This includes the latest income tax bands for England, Wales, and Northern Ireland, the separate Scottish rate bands set by the Scottish Government, and the current Class 1 National Insurance contribution rates. We also apply the Personal Allowance taper for incomes above £100,000 — where your tax-free allowance reduces by £1 for every £2 earned over the threshold, reaching zero at £125,140.

Student loan calculations follow the repayment thresholds set by the Student Loans Company for each plan type, including Plan 5 for students starting from August 2023 and the separate Postgraduate Loan scheme. Pension contributions are treated as salary sacrifice, meaning your taxable income is reduced by your contribution percentage before income tax and National Insurance are calculated. While we aim for complete accuracy, this tool is for guidance only and should not replace professional financial advice.
