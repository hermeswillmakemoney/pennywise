How we calculate this

Our calculator uses the official HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) Stamp Duty Land Tax rates and thresholds for the 2025/26 tax year, including the changes introduced in the October 2024 Budget. SDLT is calculated on a progressive slice basis — you only pay the higher rate on the portion of the price that falls within each band, not the whole amount.

First-time buyers benefit from relief on the first £425,000 of properties up to £625,000, paying no SDLT on that portion. An additional property surcharge of 3% applies to buy-to-let and second homes (on the entire purchase price for properties over £40,000). Non-UK residents pay an extra 2% surcharge on top of all other applicable rates.
